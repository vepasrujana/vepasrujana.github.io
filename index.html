How I got here

I started in applied mathematics, where I learned to love a well-posed problem. At the Indian School of Business, I sat in on classes built around case studies in attribution and hospital pricing, and saw how much a right-sized model could change a real decision. That pulled me into marketing analytics. I completed my MS in Marketing Analytics at the University of Maryland's Smith School of Business, where I also worked as a research assistant modeling Amazon review data.
